Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Music Deewane is a free, open source music player application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2. Information We Collect

Music Deewane is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information:

  • Usage Data: Anonymous, aggregated analytics to understand how the app is used (e.g., feature usage frequency). This data cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • Error Reports: Crash logs and error reports to improve app stability. These do not contain personal information.
  • Local Data: Your playlists, downloads, and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not access or transmit this data.

3. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Improve the application's features and performance
  • Fix bugs and prevent crashes
  • Understand which features are most used to prioritize development

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties. Your data stays on your device. We do not use third-party analytics services that track you across websites or applications.

5. Third-Party Services

Music Deewane may access third-party music services (YouTube Music, JioSaavn, etc.) through plugins. These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them. We do not control how these services handle your data.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect the information we collect. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Music Deewane is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Open Source

Music Deewane is open source software. You can review our complete source code on GitHub to verify our privacy practices.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our GitHub Issues or visit Shodasha.
