Your Music, Your Freedom

Music Deewane is a free, open source music player for Windows, Android, and Linux. Stream from YouTube Music, JioSaavn, and more — no ads, no tracking, no limits.

Your favourite music, all in one place.

Music Deewane gives listeners access to the world's music on a single platform. With smart playlists, synced lyrics, and offline downloads. Import your existing playlists from Spotify, YouTube, and Apple Music with a single click. Your music library, perfectly organized.

A free, open source music player that streams from YouTube Music, JioSaavn, and more. Download, organize, and listen offline — all without ads or tracking.

All new structure, familiar functionality.

Now, listeners can choose how content is displayed in their Library, jump into Search, or dive deeper into what's playing with minimal navigational disruption.

Whether you're listening, discovering, or organizing, our interface seamlessly adjusts to your current mode. Built with a plugin-based architecture, we can extend functionality through community contributions.

Explore Your Library and the Now Playing View

Your Library

Organize, Search & filter Your Library
Your Library's new design allows you to toggle through your personalized music, podcast, and audiobook collections without needing to navigate away from your current session. Filter preferences are remembered and listeners can search directly in their library for quick retrieval.

Now Playing View

This panel is all about your music. Dive into cover artwork. Save, share, and learn more about the artists, writers, and producers. View synced lyrics in real-time. Manage your queue and upcoming tracks.
